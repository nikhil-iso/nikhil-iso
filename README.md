## About Me

Hi, I'm Nikhil. I am an electrical engineering student at the University of Saskatchewan interested in mechatronics, electronics, aerospace, and hands-on engineering design.

I enjoy building physical systems that move, sense, respond, and solve real problems. My work often combines mechanical design, electronics, embedded systems, and prototyping.

I have a strong background in CAD and engineering design, with experience using Fusion 360, SolidWorks, AutoCAD, and Revit. I am also continuing to build my programming and embedded systems skills to better connect the mechanical and electrical sides of my projects.

## Featured Work

### USST Rocket UP

Through the University of Saskatchewan Space Design Team, I have contributed to high power rocket design, manufacturing, and testing, with a focus on propulsion systems.

### ARTS

ARTS is a personal passion project, short for Amateur Rocketry Telemetry System. It is my personal flight computer project for logging rocketry telemetry and flight data. The project is built around a Teensy 4.1 with an array of sensors, and is being developed step by step through individual hardware tests before combining everything into a full flight logging system.

ARTS reflects the kind of engineering I enjoy most: practical embedded systems, careful testing, and building hardware that can eventually fly, collect data, and be improved.
