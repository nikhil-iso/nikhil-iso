# Nikhil Patel

## About Me

Hi, I'm Nikhil. I am an electrical engineering student at the University of Saskatchewan with a strong interest in mechatronics, electronics, aerospace, and hands-on engineering design.

I enjoy building things that move, sense, respond, and solve real problems. A lot of my work sits between mechanical design, electronics, embedded systems, and prototyping. I am especially interested in projects where I can take an idea from a rough concept to a physical system that can be tested, improved, and used.

My strongest background is in CAD, 3D modeling, and engineering design. I have experience with Fusion 360, SolidWorks, AutoCAD, and Revit, and I enjoy using these tools to design parts, assemblies, enclosures, and full systems. I am also actively building my programming and embedded systems skills so I can better connect the mechanical and electrical sides of my projects.

Outside of school, I enjoy working on radio controlled systems, model rockets, electronics projects, and anything that gives me a chance to learn by building.

## Featured Work

### USST Rocket UP

One of the projects I am most proud of is my work with the University of Saskatchewan Space Design Team. Through USST, I have had the chance to contribute to high power rocketry, propulsion design, avionics planning, manufacturing, testing, and team-based engineering work.

This project has taught me a lot about engineering under real constraints. Design decisions have to consider performance, safety, cost, manufacturing time, reliability, and the people who will actually build and operate the system.

### Simulight Sunrise Alarm Clock

Simulight is a personal project focused on building a sunrise alarm clock that uses light to create a more natural wake-up experience. It combines electronics, programming, enclosure design, and user-focused product thinking.

This project reflects the kind of engineering I enjoy most: practical, physical, and built around improving something in everyday life.
